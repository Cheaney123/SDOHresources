Before You Send: 6 Checks
  1. Plan benefits first. Medicare Advantage, SutterSelect and Sutter Health Plan members may already have rides, post-discharge meals or an OTC allowance.
  2. Medi-Cal-only resources are marked. Medicare-only patients usually start with the Area Agency on Aging or statewide lines; the county mental health line mainly serves Medi-Cal for ongoing care.
  3. Medication costs go to the pharmacy team (HealthWell, then Cost Plus Drugs, then the manufacturer program).
  4. Mood: routine positive screens go to AI-CBT. Behavioral health outliers and complex SDOH needs go to the social worker.
  5. Paratransit can take up to 21 days to approve. For sooner appointments, use Medi-Cal rides or the plan's ride benefit.
  6. Yellow dot: a detail isn't fully verified. Confirm it before sending.
